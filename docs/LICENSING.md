# Licensing — what may be bundled, from where, and how to say so

Verified at primary sources on 29 Aug 2026 (full report: `docs/research/03-corpus-licensing.md`).

## The rule

Bundle only **System Reference Document** text under **CC-BY-4.0**, obtained from a pinned,
hash-verified structured source, with the prescribed attribution sentence and a
modification notice. Anything else is either a user's own import (never redistributed) or
out of scope.

## Attribution (exact; do not reword)

> This work includes material taken from the System Reference Document 5.1 ("SRD 5.1") by
> Wizards of the Coast LLC and available at
> https://dnd.wizards.com/resources/systems-reference-document. The SRD 5.1 is licensed
> under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License available at
> https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.

Plus, from the same legal page: *"Please do not include any other attribution regarding
Wizards other than that provided above. You may, however, include a statement on your work
that it is 'compatible with fifth edition' or '5E compatible.'"* — so **no** "™ Wizards of
the Coast" line, no Fan Content Policy disclaimer.

CC-BY-4.0 §3(a)(1)(B) requires indicating modifications; `assets/legal/ATTRIBUTION.md`
(generated by `pipeline/legal.py`) carries that notice and is rendered verbatim on the About
screen and reproduced in `README.md`.

## Naming and wording

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Grimoire", "5E compatible", "System Reference Document", "SRD 5.1", "Game Master"/"GM" | "D&D", "Dungeons & Dragons", "DnD", "Dungeon Master"/"DM" as product terms, "PHB", "Monster Manual", "Forgotten Realms", the ampersand-dragon, red/black trade dress |
| quote SRD text verbatim (a few literal "D&D"s occur inside SRD prose — leave them) | put "D&D" in the label, description, README title, or screen chrome |

MIT (inherited from `lightphone/light-sdk`; `LICENSES/MIT.txt`). Data licence CC-BY-4.0

## Process

1. New content source → add a row here **and** to `_ALLOWED` in
   `pipeline/mcp/compendium_server.py` → pin commit + hashes in `pipeline/sources.lock.json`.
2. The `licensing-auditor` agent reviews any PR touching `pipeline/sources.lock.json`,
   `pipeline/normalize/`, `tool/src/main/assets/`, or `docs/LICENSING.md`.
3. `WebFetch(domain:5e.tools)` is denied in `.claude/settings.json` so nobody "just checks"
   the wrong source into the pipeline.
